How should a voice change shape?

Compare natural Kokoro vowel transitions with reconstruction, blends and moving-resonance filters. These are cropped vowel nuclei from eighteen fresh words, not complete generated words.

All candidates receive the reference pitch, duration and loudness. The final diagnostic also receives its complete spectral envelope. Those supplied controls do not demonstrate independent speech generation. No alternative has been accepted as a quality improvement.

Equal RMS within each set · one constant gain · no limiter · no autoplay. Raw over-range samples remain recorded in the experiment; playback files have headroom. Only one player runs at a time.
